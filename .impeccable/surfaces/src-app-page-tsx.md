# Homepage surface brief

## Job

Position Codizzz as a custom digital product agency for organizations with a real operational need. The page should make the visitor feel that Codizzz studies how their business works, then engineers the appropriate digital product around it.

## Direction contract

- OWN-WORLD: A digital pattern room where requirements are translated into made-to-fit systems. Warm paper, graphite, cutting tables, plotter rails, measured contours, and precise orange registration marks create the world.
- AVOID-WORLD: Generic dark AI agency glow, star fields, glass cards, bento dashboards, invented proof, emoji icons, stock SaaS diagrams, or a cyberpunk command center.
- PALETTE: Light ground #F5F2EB with graphite #12100E and one active accent #FF8800. Dark ground #100F0E with warm paper text #F5F2EB and one active accent #E72700.
- IDENTITY: The exact supplied orange mark is shown in light mode and the exact supplied red mark in dark mode. Logo geometry is never recolored or recreated.
- TYPE: One condensed industrial display face for large statements and one neutral sans-serif for navigation, body, and controls. Maximum two families.
- MOTION: Physical, measured motion. Cut-line drawing, paper-mask reveals, and short spring feedback. Motion clarifies relationships and yields to reduced-motion settings.

## FORM

- Concept-roll seed key: `c8cf68b6`
- Dominant form: one asymmetric paper-pattern silhouette spanning the fabrication field, crossed by a single measured requirement-to-outcome contour.
- Supporting forms: a clipped intake ticket, slim plotter rails, square registration marks, and editorial rules.
- Composition behavior: the headline overlaps the quiet edge of the immersive fabrication field; the ticket overlaps the lower image edge; the service ribbon closes the first viewport.
- Repetition rule: carry registration marks, contour fragments, paper joins, or measuring rails into later surfaces, never generic decorative grids.

## Approved composition

The homepage begins with a slim horizontal navigation. The hero uses an oversized left-anchored statement over an immersive fabrication-table image. A requirement ticket overlaps the lower-left edge and asks three plain-language business questions. A six-item service ribbon closes the first viewport. On narrower screens the statement, image, ticket, and ribbon become a deliberate vertical sequence.

## Component grammar

- Corners: mostly square; small 2 to 6px radii only where the paper ticket or controls need handling affordance.
- Lines: 1px graphite rules on light surfaces, warm translucent rules on dark surfaces, 2px accent lines only for an active path.
- Elevation: overlap and material contrast, not generic drop-shadow cards.
- Controls: uppercase labels, rectangular silhouettes, minimum 44px hit areas, arrow movement limited to hover/focus feedback.
- Sections: editorial fields divided by rules and labels, not rounded card grids.

## Truth and exclusions

No statistics, testimonials, client logos, case-study outcomes, awards, ratings, pricing, availability claims, or founding dates may be shown unless the user supplies them. Founder identity is limited to publicly verified details. The physical craft metaphor expresses customization; it is not a claim about manufacturing.
