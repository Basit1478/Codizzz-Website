# Admin surface brief

## Job

Let the Codizzz owner securely add, edit, publish, reorder and remove services, career roles and team members without touching code. Team records include portrait upload. Public pages must keep working if managed content is unavailable.

## Direction contract

- THESIS: A content control desk makes the three editable resources obvious and refuses a generic analytics dashboard.
- OWN-WORLD: Warm pattern paper, graphite ink, exact registration orange, square controls, ruled rows and dense Manrope UI extend the Codizzz system into an operational surface.
- STORY: Sign in, choose Services, Careers or Team, find a record, edit it safely, then publish or remove it with explicit feedback.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact left resource rail frames a status overview, direct Add actions and a recent-content ledger; no charts or vanity metrics.
- FORM: Content Control Desk, assigned surface candidate 7, seed `c8e5ee4e`; focused resource workspaces use stable list-and-editor views.
- FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Constraints

Supabase owns authentication, database persistence and team-image storage. Only the confirmed admin identity may mutate content. Destructive actions require explicit confirmation. Mobile collapses navigation before content and preserves source/focus order.
