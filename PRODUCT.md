## Platform

web

## Users

Codizzz serves clients across industries and locations who need a digital product or AI Automation tailored to a real business requirement. The audience can include startups, established businesses, teams, and individual operators; the defining factor is a concrete digital need rather than a single company size or geography.

## Product Purpose

Codizzz designs and builds useful digital products around each client's specific needs. The website should help a prospective client quickly understand what Codizzz can build, see how an engagement moves from requirement to delivery, and start a project conversation.

## Positioning

Codizzz is a need-led digital product partner: it translates a client's operational or product requirement into a tailored AI, AI Automation, web, mobile, or software solution instead of selling a fixed off-the-shelf package.

## Operating Context

Prospective clients arrive with a business problem, workflow bottleneck, product idea, or software requirement. They evaluate Codizzz through the website, review relevant capabilities, and contact the team to discuss scope and delivery.
